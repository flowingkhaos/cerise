This blog is a demo you can touch. On the sign-in page you will find a visitor account, its
username and password written in plain sight. That is not a mistake: they are there for you.

## What you can do

With the visitor account, you see exactly what the admin sees:

- the list of every post, published and drafts;
- the editor, with its shortcuts, callouts and images;
- the publishing form: title, address, summary, topics, cover.

Change a title. Rewrite a paragraph. Add a callout. Press **Publish**.

## What happens then

A message appears: it is a demo, nothing was saved. Your text stays on screen, so you can keep
playing. But the site itself does not change. The next visitor sees exactly what you saw when you
arrived.

<Callout type="attention">
Do not type real information into the demo: no client names, no phone numbers. Nothing is kept,
but it is a good habit.
</Callout>

## Why it works this way

A public demo attracts all kinds of visits. If anyone could publish, the blog would be
unrecognisable within the hour. The visitor account lets you take the wheel, but nobody
drives off with the car.

On your own site, of course, Publish publishes.
