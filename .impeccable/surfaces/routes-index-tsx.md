Scope: the public home (`/` and `/en`), the world-setting surface for the whole blog. Visitor
mode: Persuade. Article pages are Read and the admin is Operate; both inherit this world.

Audience: beauty and care pros in Quebec, on a phone between two clients. Job: see a post worth
reading, read it, then try the editor. Action: "Lire l'article" on the newest post; second
action: open the editor with the visitor login printed on the page. Proof: the page's own speed
(measured, never claimed) and the editor itself. Constraint: no invented claims, no beauty-advice
claims, photos generated under a 75¢ cap with provenance.

and a lipstick-red tester rail beside it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every post launches like a new lipstick shade — one glossy object in sunlit film, a shade
number, a spec line. It refuses the beauty blog's white page, blush band, thin serif and three
equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Lipstick red (#C8102E) owns the masthead field and the tester rail; sky blue lives only
in photographs; butter yellow is the italic accent phrase; chrome (#E7E4E0) carries metadata and
the editor module. Bold rounded display, an italic high-contrast serif for one phrase per title,
a small uppercase mono spec line. Photos hard-cropped, bleeding off module edges, never framed.
Raises: articles flip to their markdown source (from Coptic binding); empty topics and empty
drafts render as empty tester slots (from seven-segment); one colour per job (from orienteering);
"nouveau" is the only lit state (from the BBS); hard crops (from décollage).

STORY: The reader sees a post worth reading, reads it in seconds, notices it was instant, and
opens the editor to see how it was made.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left two thirds, the newest post's photo full-bleed, wordmark "cerise" top left,
nav top right, title bottom left at about 86px with the accent phrase in italic butter yellow,
mono spec line, white pill "Lire l'article". Right third, a red tester rail: "Nouveautés", three
slots (square photo, N° in italic serif, title, spec), then a chrome module "Essayez l'éditeur"
with the visitor login and a red pill.

FORM: Lipstick Lookbook, candidate 7 of 7 on the grounded list (lipstick advertising and
beauty-counter lookbooks); seed key 317c44f4; code-led.
